Hunter Safety FAQs

What is a hunter safety course?
A hunter safety course is designed to teach the fundamentals of safe and ethical hunting. Hunter education aims to reduce the risks of a hunting incident and increase the safety of hunting for all.

Who needs to take a hunter safety course?
Generally, hunter safety courses are required for most age groups hunting public land. The requirement varies by state, so check your state law. You can do so by selecting your state above.

How long does it take to complete the online hunter safety course?
The course typically ranges from 3 to 6 hours if taken online. The online format allows users to complete it at their own pace, often with the ability to start and stop as needed. Set aside a weekend for in-person courses.

What is the cost of the online hunter safety course?
The cost can vary depending on the provider. Most online courses are $30 – $60. Recademics is $39.95 and comes with a pass guarantee. If you don’t pass, you get your money back.

Is there an exam at the end of the hunter safety course?
Yes, to receive a hunter education certificate, you must pass a final exam at the end of the course. The passing score is typically 80%. Sometimes, the final exam will be end-of-chapter quizzes scored in aggregate.

How do I get my hunter safety certificate after completing the course?

After completing the course and passing the final exam, you will receive your hunter safety certificate by email, download, or mail, depending on the state’s process. Recademics offers instant certification, allowing you to download a digital certificate.

Does the hunter safety certificate expire?

The hunter safety certificate does not expire and is valid for life.

Can I retake the hunter safety course if I fail the exam?
Yes, most course providers allow you to retake the exam if you do not pass on your first attempt. Recademics offers a pass guarantee. You can retake the exam and course for free if you fail the first time or get your money back.

What if I lose my Hunter education card?
If you lose your hunter education card, you should contact the provider that issued your certificate or the state in which you were certified. There may be a process and fee for issuing a replacement.
